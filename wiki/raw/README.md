# Raw Sources

Store a source here only when its retention is approved and useful for durable
knowledge. Preserve its original substance and add an origin note when the source,
capture date, confirmation date, or access context is not already clear.

Treat retained source records as immutable evidence. If something must be corrected
or superseded, add a new record or adjacent origin note that points to the original;
do not silently rewrite history. A canonical workspace file may be linked directly
instead of copied.

Do not store secrets, credentials, private message bodies, sensitive exports, or
material the owner has not approved for retention. A stable pointer is often safer
than a copied source. Follow the sourcing and privacy rules in
[the wiki schema](../SCHEMA.md).
